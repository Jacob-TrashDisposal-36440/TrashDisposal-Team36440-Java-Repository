Who We Are

Engineering with purpose.

Trash Disposal #36440 is an Ottawa-based FTC robotics team of 10 students in Grades 7–12. We compete in the FIRST Tech Challenge — a global program where students design, build, program, and operate robots in head-to-head competition. Beyond the robot, we develop engineering notebooks, pursue community outreach, and compete for design and innovation awards.

🔩

Build

We use the REV DUO mechanical system to design and construct competition robots capable of completing complex field challenges under time pressure.

💻

Program

Students write Java-based autonomous and driver-controlled code using the REV Control Hub and OnBot Java environment — real engineering, not toy blocks.

📓

Document

Every session is recorded in our Engineering Notebook — a judged deliverable that tracks design decisions, iterations, and lessons learned all season.

🤝

Outreach

We run community outreach events to inspire younger students in STEM and actively promote the values of gracious professionalism across Ottawa.

2026–2027 Season

BIOBUZZ™

The 2026–27 FIRST Tech Challenge game is BIOBUZZ™, presented by RTX. Teams compete on a 12×12 ft field, completing game-specific tasks during autonomous and driver-controlled periods. Our season runs from September 2026 through the Ontario Provincial Championship in February 2027.
